How TravelMate Works

Turn vague travel ideas into an organized, map-connected itinerary in three effortless steps.

1. Enter Trip Details

Specify your starting city, destination, dates, traveler count, budget and personal interests like heritage, food, or nature.

2. Let AI Create Itinerary

Our intelligent engine schedules visits chronologically, factors in local transit times, recommends meal stops, and checks weather forecasts.

3. Customize & Manage

Fine-tune stops in the Wanderlog-inspired split workspace with interactive maps, compare transport options, track expenses, and export.
